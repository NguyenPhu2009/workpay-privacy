WorkPay Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 5, 2026

WorkPay respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the WorkPay mobile application.

1. Information entered into WorkPay

WorkPay allows users to enter information such as work shifts, start and end times, break duration, hourly wages, overtime, working hours, and estimated earnings.

This information is used to provide the core features of the app. Work-related records are stored locally on the user's device.

2. Advertising and consent

WorkPay uses Google AdMob to display advertisements and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) to manage advertising consent and privacy choices where required.

Google and its advertising partners may process information such as device information, advertising identifiers, IP address, consent choices, and interactions with advertisements for purposes including advertising, fraud prevention, measurement, and related services.

For more information about how Google processes data when its services are used in apps, visit:

How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services

3. Purchases and subscriptions

WorkPay may offer a paid subscription called WorkPay Pro through Google Play.

WorkPay uses Google Play Billing to process subscription purchases. Payments and payment information are processed by Google Play. WorkPay does not receive or store users' full payment card details.

WorkPay may receive information about the status of a subscription, such as whether a WorkPay Pro subscription is active, in order to enable or disable Pro features inside the application.

Subscriptions may automatically renew according to the terms shown during the purchase process in Google Play. Users can manage or cancel their subscriptions through their Google Play account.

For more information about Google Play payments and subscriptions, please refer to Google's applicable terms and privacy policies.

4. Notifications

WorkPay may request permission to display notifications. Notification permission is used only to display notifications related to app functionality when applicable.

5. How information is used

Information is used to:

  • calculate working hours and paid hours;
  • calculate estimated earnings;
  • manage work shifts and breaks;
  • provide WorkPay application features;
  • determine whether WorkPay Pro features should be enabled;
  • display and manage advertising and advertising consent.

6. Data sharing

WorkPay does not sell users' work records.

Third-party services used by the app, including Google AdMob, Google UMP, and Google Play Billing, may process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable consent choices.

7. Data retention and deletion

Work-related data stored locally by WorkPay remains on the device until the user deletes the relevant records, clears the application's data, or uninstalls the application.

Subscription-related information processed by Google Play and information processed by other third-party services may be retained according to those providers' respective policies.

8. Security

WorkPay takes reasonable measures to minimize unnecessary collection of user information and to keep application data limited to what is required for its features.

9. Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when WorkPay features, third-party services, or data practices change. The latest version will always be available on this page.
